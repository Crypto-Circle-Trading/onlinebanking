Privacy Policy

Prototype Notice: This website is a demonstration prototype. It does not provide real banking services and does not process real financial transactions.

1. Information in the Prototype

Information displayed in this prototype, including account names, balances, transaction records, and other account details, is demonstration data.

2. Financial Information

This prototype does not connect to a bank account, cryptocurrency exchange, payment processor, or other financial institution. No real deposits, transfers, or withdrawals are processed.

3. Login Information

The current prototype uses demonstration login functionality. It should not be used for real passwords or sensitive account credentials.

4. Contact Information

Information entered into the prototype contact form is not currently transmitted to a real support system.

5. Future Version

If this project is developed into a real financial service, appropriate security, privacy, legal, regulatory, identity verification, and data-protection requirements must be addressed before launch.

6. Updates

This prototype privacy notice may be updated as the application is developed.
